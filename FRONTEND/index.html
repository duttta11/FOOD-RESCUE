How FoodRescue Works

Modeled after modern on-demand delivery logistics, tailored for private zero-waste food rescue.

01

Post Surplus Food

Donors quickly register excess cooked meals, baked goods, or grocery staples with quantity and expiry timeline.

02

Private Matching

Our internal C++ matching engine automatically pairs available donations with verified NGO requirements based on food type and urgency.

03

Dijkstra Route & Dispatch

The system calculates the optimal shortest road path using Dijkstra's algorithm and automatically dispatches an available volunteer.

04

Live Tracking & Delivery

Both donor and NGO track the rescue mission through 4 distinct stages: Assigned, Picked Up, In Transit, and Safely Delivered.

Core Technology

Why Private Matching Matters

Unlike public donation boards where uncoordinated donors and NGOs must manually call each other, FoodRescue acts as an intelligent dispatch coordinator, exactly like Swiggy or Zomato handles meal dispatch.

⚡
No Public Browsing: Donors cannot see other donors' records; NGOs cannot see other NGOs' requests.
🛣️
Dijkstra Shortest Path: Our C++ graph routing engine computes the shortest path along the city road graph to ensure hot food arrives rapidly.
🔒
Cryptographic Security: PBKDF2 password hashing with OpenSSL and server-side token authorization protects every record.
